Vehicle System Requirements Specification

Version 1.2 · VehicleSpec

1 Introduction

This document specifies the top-level requirements for the electric vehicle platform.

1.1 Scope

Covers the propulsion and chassis subsystems. Excludes:

  • infotainment
  • charging infrastructure

2 Performance Requirements

Performance requirements are verified on the standard test rig unless stated otherwise.|

2.1 Mass

R1massLimit
Vehicle mass shall not exceed 2000 kg.
satisfied by vehicle · verified by weighVehicle · derives R1.1
R1.1chassisMass
Chassis mass shall not exceed 600 kg.
not verified

2.2 Range
